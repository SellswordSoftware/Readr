Prefer to connect manually?

On Playdate, go to Settings → System → Reboot to Data Disk. Wait for the Playdate drive to appear on your computer, then select it below.

If it doesn’t appear, try another USB cable. Close Playdate Simulator or any app using the serial connection.

    A little closer to your next read

    One last step: safely eject your Playdate.

    Use Eject in your computer’s file manager, then exit Data Disk mode and open readr. Your books will appear in the library.

    Browser support & manual copying

    Use desktop Chrome or Edge for browser-assisted copying. Your browser must support choosing and writing to a local folder.

    To copy manually, enter Data Disk mode and place EPUBs directly in Data/com.sellswordsoftware.readr/. Launch readr once first so this folder exists. Eject the drive before opening readr again.

    The importer checks basic file format, not full book compatibility. Encrypted or unsupported EPUBs may not open in readr.
